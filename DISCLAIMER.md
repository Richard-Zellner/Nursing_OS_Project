# Disclaimer

Nursing OS and the Nurse Handoff package are educational portfolio projects. They are not medical devices, have not
been clinically validated, and are not clinical advice. Nothing here may be used for patient care.

**Data.** All patient records are synthetic. There are no real patients, names, dates of birth, record numbers or
facility records, and no employer material.

**Output.** The handoff generator formats what a record documents and keeps missing data visible as "Not documented". It
does not assess patients, recommend actions or replace a clinician's handoff.

**Affiliation.** The project is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any cited author, publisher, vendor or institution.
