  held-out split, mock-model Inspect task, CI. Codex built it; README and
  disclaimer wording were finalized under D-9.
- **Track 1 clinical spec** (`docs/clinical-spec-t1.md`): output contract,

  - heparin ACS start (Menon 2001)

  - potassium replacement (NurseBench-authored, modeled on VUMC)
- **Gold calculators:** 303 tests covering every band boundary and all 22
  edge cases. The parent hand-checked sample answers for heparin
  start/increase/decrease/hold, insulin before meals and at bedtime,
  hypoglycemia, and potassium bands and the renal hold.
- **Seeded generator** (seed 20260925): 150 items, 44 of them edge cases
  (29.3%), split 132 public and 18 held out. The held-out items stay in
  ignored `private/`. All items are `authored_by: agent`, `reviewed: false`.
